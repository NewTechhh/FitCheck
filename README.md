# FitCheck 💪

**Plan your training. Track your progress. Stay consistent.**

FitCheck is a workout split planning project designed to help users organize their gym training routine and structure their workouts more effectively.

The goal is to make workout planning simpler by helping users organize training days, muscle groups, and exercise routines in one convenient place.

## 🎯 The Problem

Planning a consistent workout routine can be confusing, especially for beginners who are unsure how to organize training days, distribute muscle groups, and build a balanced weekly split.

## 💡 The Solution

FitCheck aims to simplify workout organization by providing a dedicated way to plan training splits and structure weekly workout routines around individual goals and preferences.

## ✨ Key Objectives

- Organize weekly workout schedules.
- Structure training days around different muscle groups.
- Make workout planning easier and more accessible.
- Help users maintain consistency in their training.
- Provide a clear overview of their workout split.

## 👥 Who Is It For?

- Gym beginners
- Fitness enthusiasts
- Students balancing training with university
- People looking to organize their weekly workout routines
